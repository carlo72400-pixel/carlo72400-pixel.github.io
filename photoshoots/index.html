What this is

I'm a filmmaker and I'm building a body of portrait work, so I need people to shoot. I light and grade these like film frames, not snapshots. You get a full color set and a moody black and white set back, usually within a couple of days.

  • It's free. Nothing to buy, no package, no upsell.
  • I shoot in rounds. Could be next week, could be a month. You stay on the list either way.
  • You pick the spot and it's somewhere public.
  • Bring whoever you want. A friend, your partner, your whole group.
  • The photos are yours either way. If I want to post one I'll ask you first, and no is a fine answer.
  • About an hour. Wear what you actually wear. You have to be 18 or older.
